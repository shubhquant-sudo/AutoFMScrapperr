Start on Assumptions. Check History, then enter the yellow forecast inputs and the DCF inputs. All forecast/valuation inputs are intentionally blank. Zero and missing are different.

Statements contains the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, with supporting calculations and checks below them. DCF uses that forecast. History contains all normalized annual observations; Raw statements retains the original source lines. Sources contains exact raw files, normalized history and the field/cell maps.

If a required historical value is missing, check the original filing and complete its yellow cell on History. Never replace a missing figure with zero unless it is actually zero. Source period labels and reporting basis are as supplied.

The forecast holds other assets/liabilities constant and excludes acquisitions, disposals, stock compensation, FX and additional noncash adjustments. It does not add debt automatically to hide negative cash.
